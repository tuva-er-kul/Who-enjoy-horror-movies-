# Who enjoy horror movies?
Examining anwers of people who enjoy horror movies in a 150-questions survey. 

##Purpose
This project was carried out in connection with the examination in the course PSY-3035 Programming and Data Visualization for Researchers at UiT The Arctic University of Norway in the autumn of 2026.

##Dataset
The project utilizes the dataset "Young People Survey" from Miroslav Sabo at Kaggle (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/miroslavsabo/young-people-survey).
The dataset is obtained from a survey among young people (age 15-30) in Slovakia in 2013. Participants were recruited by statistics students at Faculty of Social
and Economic Sciences, Comenius University Bratislava.
The survey consisted of 150 questions mapping demographics, personality and preferences, phobias, health and spending habits, and views on life.

##Background and aim for project
Personally, I have never enjoyed horror movies. I end up being scared for months, having bad dreams and need to have someone else around in whatever room I am.
Therefore, I wanted to investigate the people who answered that they enjoy horror movies. I want to know if they are afraid of the dark (which I believe they are not), how often they have bad dreams (which I believe is never), and their age. I hope they are young, so that I am not the only one not enjoying horror movies as I get older.
These hypotheses are tested only for practicing data visualization, not for examining causal effects or find associations that can be generalized to other populations.
